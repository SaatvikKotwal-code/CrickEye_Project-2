\chapter*{Abstract}

\par Cricket coaching resources remain concentrated in major cities, while Tier-2 and Tier-3 academies rarely have motion-capture suites, radar, or dedicated video analysts. For much of India’s district-level coaching ecosystem, that gap is economic and logistical as much as technical: parents and academies cannot fund Hawk-Eye-class rigs, yet young batters still need structured feedback to compete nationally. \textbf{CrickEye} is a low-cost, computer-vision system that analyses net-session video from a \emph{single front-on camera}, delivering quantitative batting feedback without extra sensors---aligned with what those centres can actually deploy (a phone tripod and a laptop).

\par The pipeline runs in stages: YOLOv8-pose extracts COCO-17 keypoints per frame; bilateral wrist velocity and temporal gates detect swing events; each clip is classified by a Swin Transformer 3D Tiny model into five strokes (cover, flick, pull, straight, sweep) trained on 400 balanced YouTube clips (280/60/60 train/validation/test). Progressive fine-tuning from Kinetics-400 weights peaked at 98.3\% validation accuracy; the held-out test set reached \textbf{88.33\%} top-1 accuracy, with residual confusion mainly among straight, sweep, cover, and flick in the front-on view.

\par For each confirmed shot the system derives front-on-safe biomechanics---head stability, stance symmetry, elbow shape, footwork and plant timing, a swing-path proxy, and optional execution when ball length is known---and maps them through fixed coaching weights to a \textbf{0--10} composite score. Swing intensity and bat-speed estimates support dashboards and fatigue cues but stay outside that headline sum. An optional ball-analytics pass adds YOLO-based tracking, single-camera speed and length-zone estimates, and bounce heuristics.

\par A FastAPI backend streams analysis progress over WebSockets; the browser dashboard presents per-shot cards, session summaries, wagon-wheel and pitch views, ball insights where enabled, and role-based player/coach access (Supabase). Session logic aggregates deliveries, surfaces repeated technique issues, and issues drill-oriented alerts.

\par The implementation centralises tunable thresholds for auditability, separates authentication from the analysis worker for responsiveness, and documents limitations (monocular depth, approximate pace). Taken together, the results indicate that Indian Tier-2/3 programmes need not wait for elite infrastructure to obtain repeatable, coach-readable batting analytics---the bottleneck shifts from hardware spend to disciplined capture (steady front-on video) and interpretation of the dashboards CrickEye produces.

\par \textbf{Keywords:} Cricket coaching, computer vision, pose estimation, YOLOv8, Swin Transformer 3D, shot classification, biomechanical analysis, sports analytics, front-on camera, deep learning.

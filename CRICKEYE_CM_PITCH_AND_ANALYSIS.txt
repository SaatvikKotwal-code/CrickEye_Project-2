================================================================================

millions of youth. However, elite biomechanical analysis and Hawkeye ball

CRICKEYE SOLVES THIS DIVIDE.

CrickEye is a ground-breaking, indigenously developed Computer Vision and Deep
Learning platform that converts any standard smartphone or affordable 60-120 FPS
web camera into an elite-grade cricket biomechanics lab and Hawkeye trajectory
tracker—requiring ZERO expensive wearables, ZERO physical sensors on bats or
pads, and ZERO foreign licensing fees.

Key Achievements:
- 17-Keypoint Real-Time Skeleton Tracking (YOLOv8 Pose)
- Deep Spatial-Temporal Neural Shot Classification (Cover Drive, Straight Drive,
  Pull Shot, Flick, Sweep)
- Sub-Millimeter Ball Bounce Spot & Length Zone Identification (Yorker, Full,
  Good Length, Back of Length, Short)
- Objective 100-point Scientific Batting Technique Index (Head stability, stance
  symmetry, swing path directness, front-foot plant timing, elbow collapse)
- Instant Turnaround: 3-2-1 automated audio/visual countdown auto-records 1 delivery,
  evaluates within seconds, and generates an interactive Wagon Wheel, Pitch Map,

- Cost: Nearly 100x lower than imported commercial setups.

2. DEEP TECHNICAL & FUNCTIONAL PROJECT ANALYSIS
================================================================================

2.1 CORE INNOVATION & MISSION
-----------------------------
Most modern cricket video analytics either rely on manual tagging by expensive
video analysts or require wearable sensors (e.g., smart bat stickers) that alter
bat weight distribution and break easily.

CrickEye eliminates all hardware friction. It employs non-invasive Computer
Vision from a single front-on camera angle positioned safely at the batsman's
crease or bowling net.

2.2 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE & COMPUTER VISION PIPELINE
-----------------------------------------------------
The core engine consists of two tightly coupled neural stages:

A. Biomechanical Pose Estimation:
   - Engine: YOLOv8n-pose optimized with PyTorch (CUDA acceleration with dynamic
     CPU fallback).
   - Keypoint Resolution: Tracks 17 anatomical keypoints (Nose, Eyes, Ears,
     Shoulders, Elbows, Wrists, Hips, Knees, Ankles) across every frame.
   - Scale-Invariant Normalization: Automatically estimates batsman scale using
     median shoulder-width across frames. This ensures that metrics remain
     equally accurate whether the camera is 3 meters or 12 meters away.

B. Deep Shot Classification:
   - Architecture: Custom 3D Deep Convolutional / Swin3D action recognition
     model (`crickeye_best.pth`).
   - Classes Detected: Cover Drive, Straight Drive, Pull Shot, Flick, Sweep.
   - Output: Classification confidence percentage, temporal onset timestamp,
     start-to-finish shot execution window.

2.3 BIOMECHANICAL METRICS & ASSESSMENT SCIENCE (THE 100-POINT COMPOSITE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rather than giving vague subjective advice, CrickEye measures clinical cricket
biomechanics based on textbook batting principles formulated by BCCI/ICC coaches:

1. Head Stability (28% - 32% Weight):
   - Measures lateral head drift and vertical dipping/lifting during the
     delivery stride and at the moment of impact.
   - Diagnostic Flags: HEAD_LATERAL_DRIFT, HEAD_VERTICAL_DRIFT.

2. Footwork & Weight Transfer (20% - 24% Weight):
   - Evaluates pre-shot trigger movement, front-foot plant timing relative to
     ball bounce, and feet activity rate across the entire session.
   - Diagnostic Flags: NO_TRIGGER, LATE_PLANT, FEET_STATIC.

3. Stance Symmetry & Shoulder/Hip Tilt (12% - 14% Weight):
   - Calculates angular deviation of shoulder and hip axes from the horizontal
     plane (0° - 15° gold standard). Prevents collapsing or falling over to the
     off-side.

4. Elbow Elevation & Shape (12% - 14% Weight):
   - Analyzes high front-elbow elevation on drives and detects elbow collapse or
     tucking behind the thigh pad at impact.

5. Shot Execution & Ball-Length Alignment (10% - 22% Weight):
   - Cross-references the detected shot type with the incoming ball length zone
     (e.g., driving a full delivery = high score; pulling a full delivery = risky).

6. Swing Path Quality (6% Weight):
   - Computes wrist-path directness and bat arc fluidity from downswing to
     follow-through.

Composite Scoring: Produces a definitive Shot Score out of 10.0 and categorizes
execution into Top Class, Good, Average, or Needs Work.

2.4 BALL TRACKING & SINGLE-CAMERA HAWKEYE PHYSICS
-------------------------------------------------
Implemented in `ball_analytics.py`:
- Ball Detection: Fine-tuned YOLO ball detection (`best_ball.pt`) identifying
  the red/white cricket ball across high-speed trajectories.
- Trajectory Reconstruction: Parabolic polynomial curve fitting modeling gravity,
  air drag, and pitch bounce restitution.
- Pitch Map Zones: Classifies deliveries into 5 international length zones:
  Yorker (0-2m from stumps), Full (2-4m), Good Length (4-6m), Back of Length (6-8m),
  and Short (>8m).
- Pace & Speed Estimation: Derives release speed (km/h) based on calibrated
  22-yard pitch flight time and pixel-to-meter homography.
- Wagon Wheel Generator: Automatically projects estimated shot direction onto an
  interactive 360° cricket ground canvas.

2.5 WORKFLOW & EDGE/MOBILE ARCHITECTURE
---------------------------------------
- Android App Container (`CrickEye-Pro.apk`):
  * Native Java/WebView bridge with screen wake-lock (display remains active
    during continuous net sessions).
  * Tactile haptic countdown vibration alerting the batsman when the AI begins
    recording.
  * Front/rear camera switcher supporting standard smartphone lenses.
- Automated Practice Flow:
  * 3-2-1 Audio/Visual Countdown beep.
  * 5-second automatic delivery capture window.
  * Instant background upload over local Wi-Fi / Cloudflare tunnel.
  * WebSocket real-time progress broadcast to coach and player screens.
- Edge Readiness (Jetson Nano / Low-Cost Mini PCs):
  * Decoupled architecture: High-speed 60-120 FPS camera recording into a short
    RAM buffer, processed by low-cost edge AI at 15 FPS without losing a single
    frame of trajectory physics.

2.6 DATA SECURITY & COACH ECOSYSTEM
-----------------------------------
- Supabase PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS) isolating player records.
- Session History & Fatigue Detection: Compares performance in the first half
  versus the second half of net sessions to flag technical breakdown caused by
  stamina loss.
- AI LLM Coaching Insights: Summarizes actionable drills in plain regional language
  or English (e.g., "Keep your head 5 cm lower on full deliveries; plant front
  foot 0.1s earlier").

5.1 DEMOCRATIZING ELITE COACHING FOR RURAL & TIER-2/3 TALENT
In traditional sports ecosystems, scouts and selectors only visit major metropolitan
stadiums. A prodigy playing in a remote village or tribal taluka remains unseen
unless their family can afford relocation to a premier metro academy.
CrickEye creates a verified, unalterable digital performance record. A young player
in a remote government hostel can record their net session, receive a 9.2/10
technique score with trajectory proof, and be flagged immediately on the State
Sports Authority's scouting radar.

5.2 INJURY PREVENTION AMONG YOUNG ATHLETES
Youth bowlers and batsmen frequently suffer lumbar stress fractures, rotator cuff
tears, and knee ligament damage due to improper body alignment and over-rotation.
CrickEye's real-time detection of lateral head drift, stance collapse, and fatigue
allows coaches to correct flawed mechanics before they cause physical injury.
